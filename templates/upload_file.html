How the Diagnostic Pipeline Works

STEP 01

Schema Validation

Scans telemetry inputs to confirm 590 distinct wafer sensor features and handles missing/corrupted columns automatically.

STEP 02

Imputation & Transformation

Applies robust statistical preprocessing and median imputation to bridge missing sensor readings without introducing artificial variance.

STEP 03

XGBoost Classification

Evaluates gradient-boosted decision trees to classify each wafer as normal (-1) or defective (+1) and streams back an annotated CSV.
